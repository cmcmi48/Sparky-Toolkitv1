📐 Electrical formulas

Ohm's law

V = I × R   |   I = V / R   |   R = V / I

DC / resistive power

P = V × I   |   P = I²R   |   P = V²/R

Single-phase real power

P (W) = V × I × PF

Balanced three-phase real power

P (W) = √3 × VLL × IL × PF

Apparent power

Single phase: S (VA) = V × I
Three phase: S (VA) = √3 × VLL × IL

Power factor & reactive power

PF = P / S   |   Q = √(S² − P²)

Battery energy

Nominal Wh ≈ V × Ah
